# Code of Conduct — Typecheck

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in Typecheck a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, experience, nationality, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful, welcoming, and patient.
- No harassment, No hate, No spam. Typing is for everyone.
- Focus on what is best for the community — local, private, fast.

## Enforcement
Report to `hello@typecheck.test` or via GitHub `Report abuse`. Maintainers will review and may remove contributions.
